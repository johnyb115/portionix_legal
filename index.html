Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the application Portionix handles user data.

Personal Data

Portionix does not require user registration and does not collect or store any personally identifiable information.

Local Data Storage

The app allows users to create and manage personal settings related to food portions and dietary preferences. These settings are stored locally on the user's device in the form of configuration files (e.g. JSON). Users may optionally export these files manually. The developer has no access to this data.

Advertising

The free version of Portionix displays advertisements provided by third-party services, such as Google AdMob. AdMob may collect anonymous usage data and device identifiers for the purpose of displaying ads, in accordance with Google’s privacy policies.

Portionix does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

Children

Portionix is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
